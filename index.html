▸ Calibrate with my own application log

Enter your real totals so far. The model back-solves its baseline rates so it reproduces your observed rates at your current slider settings. Rates with too few data points are skipped, because small samples mislead.

Minimums: 40 cold apps, 15 outreach messages, 8 screens, 6 interviews.
    ▸ How the model works
    • Touchpoints = daily applications and outreach × active weekdays (2 of every 3 calendar days).
    • Screens = cold apps × cold screen rate + outreach × warm screen rate. Tailoring hours and upskilling lift both rates; sending more than ~12 apps/day or exceeding ~42 hrs/week applies a quality penalty.
    • Interviews = screens × screen-to-interview rate (raised by prep hours). With sponsorship on, only screens at employers that can sponsor count.
    • Expected offers = interviews × interview-to-offer rate. Odds of at least one offer = 1 − e^(−expected offers), a Poisson approximation that assumes independent outcomes.
    • Time to first offer adds the hiring-cycle lag to the time needed to accumulate roughly 0.7 expected offers. Offer odds describe the pipeline built in this window; offers usually land after the window ends.
    • Known limits: no seasonality, no correlation between applications, no learning effect. Treat outputs as relative comparisons between strategies, not predictions.
